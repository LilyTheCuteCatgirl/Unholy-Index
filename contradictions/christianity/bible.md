# Numerical discrepancies

- 2 Samuel 24:9 + 1 Chronicles 21:5 - David’s census gives different troop totals for Israel and Judah. The counting scope may differ. | Explanation: Samuel gives 800,000 for Israel and 500,000 for Judah; Chronicles gives 1,100,000 and 470,000 respectively. The totals differ, although the two accounts may count different groups.
- 2 Samuel 10:18 + 1 Chronicles 19:18 - The parallel accounts give 700 versus 7,000 chariot-related casualties; manuscript and translation differences matter. | Explanation: The parallel battle accounts report 700 versus 7,000 chariot-related losses. The distinction between chariots and their crews, and possible copying or translation differences, should be considered.
# Conflicting accounts

- Matthew 27:3-10 + Acts 1:18-19 - The accounts differ on Judas’s death and field purchase; a combined sequence is a possible harmonization. | Explanation: Matthew describes Judas hanging himself and the priests buying the field. Acts attributes the field to Judas and describes his fatal fall. A sequence combining both accounts is possible but is not stated in either passage.
