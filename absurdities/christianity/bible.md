# Extraordinary claims

- Joshua 10:12-14 - Reports the sun and moon standing still during a battle, presented as divine intervention. | Explanation: The narrative describes the sun and moon stopping during a battle. A literal reading involves an extraordinary astronomical event outside ordinary celestial motion; the passage presents it as a miracle, not a scientific account.
- John 11:43-44 - Reports Jesus raising Lazarus from death and calling him out of his tomb. | Explanation: The narrative presents Jesus bringing a dead man back to life. The extraordinary claim is the reversal of death, understood in the text as a miracle rather than an ordinary biological event.
- Numbers 22:28-30 - Reports God enabling Balaam’s donkey to speak and converse with him. | Explanation: The donkey speaks in human language and converses with Balaam, beyond the normal linguistic abilities of donkeys. The narrative explicitly attributes this ability to divine intervention.
# Scientific tensions

- Genesis 1:11-19 - Places vegetation before the creation of the sun; a literal chronological reading conflicts with natural history. | Explanation: Vegetation appears before the heavenly lights in the narrative sequence. A literal chronological interpretation conflicts with the scientific understanding that the sun predates plants on Earth; nonliteral readings need not make that chronological claim.
