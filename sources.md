# Sources and recovery notes

This is a new, locally packaged recovery, not the exact ZIP described in the inaccessible Deep Research report. The original 209 Bible and 251 Quran entries were accessible. Their long descriptions were edited; two additional Bible references were added. The hadith file is a partial reconstruction from the report, its Sources-tab export, and reference checks. The hadith entries have not all been independently rechecked in full, and some Shia references are chapter-level. The original report claimed 526 entries, but its later files could not be accessed from this runtime. This version does not attempt to force that count.

## Primary reference sites

- Bible: https://www.biblegateway.com/ (66-book Protestant baseline; compare NRSVUE, NIV, ESV, NKJV)
- Quran: https://quran.com/ and https://corpus.quran.com/translation.jsp
- Sunni hadith: https://sunnah.com/ (collection and number; Sunan grades where available)
- Shia hadith: https://shialibrary.com/ and https://thaqalayn.com/
- Discovery index: https://skepticsannotatedbible.com/categories.html
- Shechem: https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Genesis%2034%3A25-31%3B49%3A5-7&version=NRSVUE
- Jeremiah: https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Jeremiah%2019%3A9&version=NRSVUE
- Al-Kafi apostasy chapter: https://shialibrary.com/book/kafi/chapter/528556
- Al-Kafi sodomy chapter: https://shialibrary.com/book/kafi/chapter/528516
- Al-Kafi jointly owned enslaved woman: https://thaqalayn.com/hadith/5/2/96/2

The entries summarize morally controversial textual passages, not the beliefs or behavior of contemporary adherents. Interpretations and authentication judgments can differ. The collection includes some overlapping references under different themes and is not exhaustive. Check primary texts before public use.
