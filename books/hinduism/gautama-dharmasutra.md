# Other discriminatory rules

- Gautama Dharmasutra 10.39-44 - Assigns different privileged means of acquiring property to different social classes. | https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/gautama-dharmasutra/d/doc116310.html
- Gautama Dharmasutra 10.50-59 - Assigns Shudras service to higher classes and consumption of their leftovers and discarded goods. | https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/gautama-dharmasutra/d/doc116310.html
- Gautama Dharmasutra 10.61-66 - Requires Shudras to support impoverished masters, and all people to serve higher classes. | https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/gautama-dharmasutra/d/doc116310.html
- Gautama Dharmasutra 12.8-14 - Sets unequal fines for insults by caste, exempting Brahmins from fines for insulting Shudras. | https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/gautama-dharmasutra/d/doc116312.html
- Gautama Dharmasutra 12.46-47 - Exempts Brahmins from corporal punishment, while permitting public denunciation, exile and branding. | https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/gautama-dharmasutra/d/doc116312.html
- Gautama Dharmasutra 18.24-27 - Allows taking money by force or deceit from Shudras or specified ritual neglecters for religious expenses. | https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/gautama-dharmasutra/d/doc116318.html
- Gautama Dharmasutra 4.16-21 - Classifies children of inter-caste unions according to their parents' caste and the direction of the union. | https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/gautama-dharmasutra/d/doc116304.html
- Gautama Dharmasutra 4.25-28 - Excludes specified mixed-caste descendants from sacred law and describes one group as especially impure. | https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/gautama-dharmasutra/d/doc116304.html
- Gautama Dharmasutra 9.10-11 - Forbids sipping water offered by a Shudra or someone considered impure. | https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/gautama-dharmasutra/d/doc116309.html
- Gautama Dharmasutra 9.16-18 - Forbids conversing with people classified as outsiders or impure, prescribing corrective acts after contact. | https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/gautama-dharmasutra/d/doc116309.html

# Cruel and disproportionate punishments

- Gautama Dharmasutra 12.1 - Prescribes mutilation for a Shudra who criminally insults or assaults a twice-born man. | https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/gautama-dharmasutra/d/doc116312.html
- Gautama Dharmasutra 12.2-3 - Prescribes genital mutilation, confiscation and, under specified conditions, execution for a Shudra's prohibited sexual relations. | https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/gautama-dharmasutra/d/doc116312.html
- Gautama Dharmasutra 12.4-6 - Prescribes molten substances in the ears, tongue removal or death for Shudras learning Vedic texts. | https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/gautama-dharmasutra/d/doc116312.html
- Gautama Dharmasutra 12.7 - Prescribes corporal punishment for a Shudra claiming equal social position with twice-born men. | https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/gautama-dharmasutra/d/doc116312.html
- Gautama Dharmasutra 23.1-2 - Prescribes a fatal punishment for deliberate alcohol consumption by a Brahmin, but a lesser penance for accidental drinking. | https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/gautama-dharmasutra/d/doc116323.html
- Gautama Dharmasutra 23.8-11 - Prescribes lethal penance for sexual violation of a teacher's bed. | https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/gautama-dharmasutra/d/doc116323.html
- Gautama Dharmasutra 23.14-16 - Prescribes a brutal execution for a woman committing adultery with a lower-caste man, also punishing him. | https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/gautama-dharmasutra/d/doc116323.html

# Women's rights

- Gautama Dharmasutra 18.1-3 - Denies wives independent religious practice and requires obedience to marital duties. | https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/gautama-dharmasutra/d/doc116318.html
- Gautama Dharmasutra 18.4-8 - Restricts a widow seeking children to prescribed partners, permission requirements and at most two sons. | https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/gautama-dharmasutra/d/doc116318.html
- Gautama Dharmasutra 18.15-17 - Requires wives of absent husbands to wait for prescribed periods or abstain if husbands renounce domestic life. | https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/gautama-dharmasutra/d/doc116318.html

# Children's rights

- Gautama Dharmasutra 18.20-23 - Prescribes marrying girls before puberty; records an even earlier opinion, while allowing later self-choice under conditions. | https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/gautama-dharmasutra/d/doc116318.html

# Collective punishment and divine cruelty

- Gautama Dharmasutra 23.29-31 - Treats lying to a spiritual teacher as destroying the offender, seven ancestors and seven descendants. | https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/gautama-dharmasutra/d/doc116323.html
