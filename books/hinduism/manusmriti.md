# Slavery

- Manusmriti 8.410-414 - Assigns compulsory service to Shudras, even after emancipation. | https://hinduismfacts.org/hindu-scriptures-and-holy-books/manusmriti/chapter-viii/ | https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/manusmriti-with-the-commentary-of-medhatithi/d/doc201348.html
- Manusmriti 8.415 - Recognizes seven forms of enslavement, including capture, purchase, inheritance and punishment. | https://hinduismfacts.org/hindu-scriptures-and-holy-books/manusmriti/chapter-viii/
- Manusmriti 8.417 - Allows Brahmins to appropriate their enslaved Shudras' possessions. | https://hinduismfacts.org/hindu-scriptures-and-holy-books/manusmriti/chapter-viii/

# Women's rights

- Manusmriti 5.147-149 - Denies women independence throughout life and requires male guardianship. | https://hinduismfacts.org/hindu-scriptures-and-holy-books/manusmriti/chapter-v/
- Manusmriti 5.151-152 - Requires obedience to a husband chosen by a father or authorized brother. | https://hinduismfacts.org/hindu-scriptures-and-holy-books/manusmriti/chapter-v/
- Manusmriti 5.154 - Requires a faithful wife to revere even a morally deficient or unfaithful husband. | https://hinduismfacts.org/hindu-scriptures-and-holy-books/manusmriti/chapter-v/
- Manusmriti 5.155-156 - Restricts independent female religious observance and requires avoiding displeasing husbands. | https://hinduismfacts.org/hindu-scriptures-and-holy-books/manusmriti/chapter-v/
- Manusmriti 5.157-162 - Prescribes lifelong chastity for widows and rejects remarriage for virtuous women. | https://hinduismfacts.org/hindu-scriptures-and-holy-books/manusmriti/chapter-v/
- Manusmriti 5.164 - Threatens disobedient wives with disgrace, animal rebirth and illness. | https://hinduismfacts.org/hindu-scriptures-and-holy-books/manusmriti/chapter-v/
- Manusmriti 5.168 - Allows widowers to remarry, unlike the prescribed ideal for widows. | https://hinduismfacts.org/hindu-scriptures-and-holy-books/manusmriti/chapter-v/
- Manusmriti 8.416 - Denies independent ownership of earnings to wives, sons and slaves. | https://hinduismfacts.org/hindu-scriptures-and-holy-books/manusmriti/chapter-viii/
- Manusmriti 9.13-17 - Portrays women as naturally unfaithful and deceitful, requiring close supervision. | https://hinduismfacts.org/hindu-scriptures-and-holy-books/manusmriti/chapter-ix/
- Manusmriti 9.18 - Excludes women from specified Vedic rites and associates them with impurity. | https://hinduismfacts.org/hindu-scriptures-and-holy-books/manusmriti/chapter-ix/
- Manusmriti 9.77-79 - Allows confiscating wives' property for hostility or disrespect, with specified exceptions. | https://hinduismfacts.org/hindu-scriptures-and-holy-books/manusmriti/chapter-ix/
- Manusmriti 9.80-82 - Allows taking another wife for infertility, daughters only, illness or other conditions, with exceptions. | https://hinduismfacts.org/hindu-scriptures-and-holy-books/manusmriti/chapter-ix/
- Manusmriti 9.83-84 - Permits confining or repudiating a replaced wife who leaves angrily, and fines forbidden outings. | https://hinduismfacts.org/hindu-scriptures-and-holy-books/manusmriti/chapter-ix/
- Manusmriti 9.85 - Ranks a man's wives by their caste. | https://hinduismfacts.org/hindu-scriptures-and-holy-books/manusmriti/chapter-ix/

# Children's rights

- Manusmriti 9.88 - Recommends giving a daughter to a suitable same-caste suitor before marriageable age. | https://hinduismfacts.org/hindu-scriptures-and-holy-books/manusmriti/chapter-ix/
- Manusmriti 9.94 - Recommends marriage between men aged 30 and girls aged 12, or 24 and 8. | https://hinduismfacts.org/hindu-scriptures-and-holy-books/manusmriti/chapter-ix/ | https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/manusmriti-with-the-commentary-of-medhatithi/d/doc201459.html

# Other discriminatory rules

- Manusmriti 8.267-268 - Sets unequal defamation penalties according to caste. | https://hinduismfacts.org/hindu-scriptures-and-holy-books/manusmriti/chapter-viii/
- Manusmriti 8.374-378 - Sets caste-dependent penalties for intercourse, including death, mutilation or fines. | https://hinduismfacts.org/hindu-scriptures-and-holy-books/manusmriti/chapter-viii/
- Manusmriti 8.379-381 - Exempts Brahmins from execution, even for grave crimes. | https://hinduismfacts.org/hindu-scriptures-and-holy-books/manusmriti/chapter-viii/
- Manusmriti 10.46-50 - Assigns stigmatized occupations and separate living areas to communities classified by ancestry. | https://hinduismfacts.org/hindu-scriptures-and-holy-books/manusmriti/chapter-x/
- Manusmriti 10.51-56 - Requires segregation, restricted movement and stigmatized occupations for Chandalas and Svapakas. | https://hinduismfacts.org/hindu-scriptures-and-holy-books/manusmriti/chapter-x/
- Manusmriti 10.57-59 - Attributes allegedly impure ancestry to inherent negative character traits. | https://hinduismfacts.org/hindu-scriptures-and-holy-books/manusmriti/chapter-x/
- Manusmriti 10.126-128 - Restricts Shudras' access to sacraments and Vedic recitation. | https://hinduismfacts.org/hindu-scriptures-and-holy-books/manusmriti/chapter-x/
- Manusmriti 10.129 - Forbids Shudras from accumulating wealth, even when able to do so. | https://hinduismfacts.org/hindu-scriptures-and-holy-books/manusmriti/chapter-x/

# Cruel and disproportionate punishments

- Manusmriti 8.270-272 - Prescribes mutilation or scalding for Shudras insulting or arrogantly instructing higher castes. | https://hinduismfacts.org/hindu-scriptures-and-holy-books/manusmriti/chapter-viii/ | https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/manusmriti-with-the-commentary-of-medhatithi/d/doc201203.html
- Manusmriti 8.279-280 - Prescribes amputation for lower-caste people assaulting members of higher castes. | https://hinduismfacts.org/hindu-scriptures-and-holy-books/manusmriti/chapter-viii/
- Manusmriti 8.281 - Prescribes branding and banishment or mutilation for sharing a higher-caste person's seat. | https://hinduismfacts.org/hindu-scriptures-and-holy-books/manusmriti/chapter-viii/
- Manusmriti 8.282-283 - Prescribes mutilation for specified insults or physical contact against caste superiors. | https://hinduismfacts.org/hindu-scriptures-and-holy-books/manusmriti/chapter-viii/
- Manusmriti 8.299-300 - Permits beating wives, sons, slaves, pupils and younger brothers for faults. | https://hinduismfacts.org/hindu-scriptures-and-holy-books/manusmriti/chapter-viii/
- Manusmriti 8.371-372 - Prescribes public execution of an adulterous wife and burning of the male offender. | https://hinduismfacts.org/hindu-scriptures-and-holy-books/manusmriti/chapter-viii/

# LGBT discrimination

- Manusmriti 8.369-370 - Prescribes fines, whipping or mutilation for sexual acts between females, depending on age. | https://hinduismfacts.org/hindu-scriptures-and-holy-books/manusmriti/chapter-viii/

# Sexual slavery, forced marriage and sexual violence

- Manusmriti 8.378 - Prescribes only a fine for a Brahmin raping a guarded Brahmin woman. | https://hinduismfacts.org/hindu-scriptures-and-holy-books/manusmriti/chapter-viii/
