# Sunni - Children and marriage

- Sahih al-Bukhari 5133 - Reports Aisha married at six and the marriage was consummated at nine.
- Sunan an-Nasa'i 3255 [Sahih] - Reports Aisha's marriage at six and consummation at nine.

# Sunni - Women's rights

- Sahih al-Bukhari 304 - Describes women as deficient in intelligence and religion, and numerous among hell's inhabitants.
- Sahih al-Bukhari 5193 - Says angels curse a wife until morning for refusing her husband's bed.
- Sahih al-Bukhari 7099 - Says a nation led by a woman will not prosper.
- Sunan Abi Dawud 2147 [Da'if] - Says husbands will not be questioned about why they beat their wives.
- Sahih al-Bukhari 5825 - Reports a woman's bruises and complaint concerning her husband.
- Sahih al-Bukhari 5096 - Reports an association of bad omens with women, houses and horses.
- Sahih Muslim 510a - Describes women, donkeys and black dogs as interrupting prayer under specified conditions.
- Sahih Muslim 1468a - Compares women to ribs and advises kind treatment while describing their perceived crookedness.

# Sunni - Slavery and sexual slavery

- Sahih Muslim 1456a - Reports captured married women becoming sexually permissible after capture under the stated conditions.
- Sahih Muslim 1438a - Reports soldiers taking captive women and discussing withdrawal during intercourse.
- Sahih al-Bukhari 2542 - Reports captives and consultation concerning withdrawal during intercourse with enslaved women.
- Sahih al-Bukhari 5083 - Discusses marriage and manumission of enslaved women.

# Sunni - Religious intolerance

- Sahih al-Bukhari 6922 - Reports an execution command for leaving Islam.
- Sahih al-Bukhari 6923 - Reports execution of a man who returned to Judaism after converting to Islam.
- Sahih Muslim 1767a - Reports Muhammad's intention to expel Jews and Christians from the Arabian Peninsula.
- Sahih Muslim 21a - Reports a command to fight until people profess faith and fulfill specified religious duties.
- Sahih Muslim 2922 - Predicts an end-times battle in which Muslims kill Jews.

# Sunni - LGBT discrimination

- Sunan Abi Dawud 4462 [Hasan Sahih, al-Albani] - Prescribes death for participants in acts associated with Lot's people.
- Sahih al-Bukhari 5885 - Reports curses against men imitating women and women imitating men.

# Sunni - Cruel and disproportionate punishments

- Sahih Muslim 1691a - Reports Muhammad's stoning of married adulterers and Umar's defense of that punishment.
- Sahih al-Bukhari 6814 - Reports Muhammad ordering a married man stoned for adultery.
- Sahih Muslim 1695a - Reports stoning a woman for adultery after childbirth and a delay to provide childcare.
- Sahih Muslim 1671a - Reports severe mutilation and punishment of men accused of murder and theft.

# Sunni - Warfare

- Sahih al-Bukhari 3012 - Reports acceptance of incidental deaths of women and children in night raids.

# Sunni - Animal cruelty

- Sahih Muslim 1572 - Reports an earlier order to kill dogs, later narrowed, including specified black dogs.
- Sahih Muslim 2240a - Promises greater reward for killing a gecko with fewer blows.

# Shia - Religious intolerance

- Al-Kafi, vol. 7, Chapter on the Punishment for Apostasy [grading varies] - Contains reports prescribing execution for apostasy under specified conditions.

# Shia - LGBT discrimination

- Al-Kafi, vol. 7, Chapter on the Punishment for Sodomy [grading varies] - Contains reports prescribing severe punishments for male same-sex acts.
- Al-Kafi, vol. 7, Chapter on the Punishment for Lesbianism [grading varies] - Contains restrictions on women sharing bedding without a barrier.

# Shia - Slavery and sexual slavery

- Al-Kafi, vol. 5, book 2, chapter 96, hadith 2 [grading varies] - Adjusts punishment for intercourse with a jointly owned enslaved woman according to ownership share.
- Al-Kafi, vol. 5, A Woman's Marriage with Her Slave [grading varies] - Reports flogging a woman and her enslaved man for intercourse, then selling him.
- Al-Khisal 10-27 [grading varies] - Lists categories of enslaved women temporarily or permanently forbidden for intercourse with owners.
