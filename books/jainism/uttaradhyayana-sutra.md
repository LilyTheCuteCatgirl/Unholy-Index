# Women's rights

- Uttaradhyayana Sutra 8.18-19 - Portrays women as deceptive demonic beings and instructs homeless monks to avoid them. | https://www.wisdomlib.org/jainism/book/uttaradhyayana-sutra/d/doc424218.html
- Uttaradhyayana Sutra 32.13-16 - Compares monks living near women to mice near cats, recommending avoidance of women's appearance and company. | https://www.wisdomlib.org/jainism/book/uttaradhyayana-sutra/d/doc424242.html
- Uttaradhyayana Sutra 32.17-18 - Depicts attachment to women as the greatest obstacle for men seeking spiritual liberation. | https://www.wisdomlib.org/jainism/book/uttaradhyayana-sutra/d/doc424242.html
