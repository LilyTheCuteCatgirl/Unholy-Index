# Women's rights

- Anguttara Nikaya 8.51 - Subordinates nuns to monks and predicts women's ordination will shorten the teaching's survival. | https://suttacentral.net/an8.51/en/sujato
- Majjhima Nikaya 115 - States that women cannot become fully awakened Buddhas or universal monarchs in female form. | https://suttacentral.net/mn115/en/sujato
- Anguttara Nikaya 5.229-230 - Compares women to dangerous snakes and generalizes about their alleged negative qualities. | https://find.dhamma.gift/bw/an/an5.229.html | https://find.dhamma.gift/bw/an/an5.230.html

# Cruel and disproportionate punishments

- Majjhima Nikaya 130 - Describes prolonged hell torments resulting from bad deeds, ending when the relevant karma is exhausted. | https://suttacentral.net/mn130/en/sujato
