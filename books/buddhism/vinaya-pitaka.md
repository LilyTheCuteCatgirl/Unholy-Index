# Slavery

- Mahavagga I.47.1 - Bars enslaved people from entering the monastic order while enslaved. | https://www.dhammatalks.org/vinaya/bmc/Section0054.html

# Other discriminatory rules

- Mahavagga I.39.7 - Bars people with five specified diseases, including epilepsy, from monastic entry. | https://www.dhammatalks.org/vinaya/bmc/Section0054.html
- Mahavagga I.46.1 - Bars debtors from entering the monastic order. | https://www.dhammatalks.org/vinaya/bmc/Section0054.html

# LGBT discrimination

- Mahavagga I.61.2 - Bars ordination of pandakas, an ancient sexual or gender category without an exact modern equivalent. | https://www.dhammatalks.org/vinaya/bmc/Section0054.html | https://www.wisdomlib.org/buddhism/book/vinaya-2-the-mahavagga/d/doc370054.html
- Mahavagga I.68 - Bars ordination of people described as having both sexual characteristics, requiring expulsion if ordained. | https://www.dhammatalks.org/vinaya/bmc/Section0054.html

# Religious intolerance

- Mahavagga I.62.3 - Bars reordination of former monks who joined another religion while ordained. | https://www.dhammatalks.org/vinaya/bmc/Section0054.html
