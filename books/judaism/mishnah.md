# Slavery

- Mishnah Kiddushin 1:2 - Regulates acquiring Hebrew slaves and their release, including extended service by ear piercing. | https://www.sefaria.org/Mishnah_Kiddushin.1.2?lang=en
- Mishnah Kiddushin 1:3 - Regulates purchase, possession and emancipation of non-Jewish slaves termed Canaanite slaves. | https://www.sefaria.org/Mishnah_Kiddushin.1.3?lang=en

# Women's rights

- Mishnah Ketubot 4:4 - Gives fathers authority over daughters' betrothal, earnings and annulment of vows. | https://www.sefaria.org/Mishnah_Ketubot.4.4?lang=en
- Mishnah Ketubot 4:5 - Describes transfer of a daughter from paternal authority to her husband's authority. | https://www.sefaria.org/Mishnah_Ketubot.4.5?lang=en
- Mishnah Sotah 1:5 - Prescribes public humiliation of wives suspected of adultery, with disagreement over limits. | https://www.sefaria.org/Mishnah_Sotah.1.5?lang=en
- Mishnah Sotah 3:4 - Rabbi Eliezer condemns teaching daughters Torah; Ben Azzai argues for teaching them. | https://www.sefaria.org/Mishnah_Sotah.3.4?lang=en

# Children's rights

- Mishnah Niddah 5:4 - Recognizes betrothal through intercourse with girls aged three years and one day; later restrictions exist. | https://www.sefaria.org/Mishnah_Niddah.5.4?lang=en | https://www.sefaria.org/Kiddushin.41a?lang=en
- Mishnah Sanhedrin 8:1-5 - Regulates executing rebellious sons under narrow conditions, anticipating future wrongdoing; later rabbis disputed applicability. | https://www.sefaria.org/Mishnah_Sanhedrin.8.1-5?lang=en | https://www.sefaria.org/Sanhedrin.71a?lang=en

# LGBT discrimination

- Mishnah Sanhedrin 7:4 - Lists male same-sex intercourse among offenses punishable by stoning. | https://www.sefaria.org/Mishnah_Sanhedrin.7.4?lang=en

# Religious intolerance

- Mishnah Sanhedrin 7:5 - Specifies evidentiary conditions for a capital prosecution for blasphemy. | https://www.sefaria.org/Mishnah_Sanhedrin.7.5?lang=en
- Mishnah Sanhedrin 7:6 - Defines forms of idol worship punishable by death. | https://www.sefaria.org/Mishnah_Sanhedrin.7.6?lang=en
- Mishnah Sanhedrin 7:10 - Permits concealed witnesses to obtain evidence against an inciter to idol worship. | https://www.sefaria.org/Mishnah_Sanhedrin.7.10?lang=en

# Warfare and mass killing

- Mishnah Sanhedrin 10:4-5 - Regulates execution of idolaters and destruction of their city's property; applicability is disputed in later discussions. | https://www.sefaria.org/Mishnah_Sanhedrin.10.4-5?lang=en | https://www.sefaria.org/Sanhedrin.71a?lang=en

# Cruel and disproportionate punishments

- Mishnah Sanhedrin 7:1-3 - Defines four judicial execution methods, including stoning and burning. | https://www.sefaria.org/Mishnah_Sanhedrin.7.1-3?lang=en | https://www.sefaria.org/Mishnah_Makkot.1.10?lang=en
