# Women's rights

- Mishneh Torah, Testimony 9:2 - Disqualifies women from ordinary judicial testimony under the stated scriptural interpretation. | https://www.sefaria.org/Mishneh_Torah%2C_Testimony.9.2?lang=en
- Mishneh Torah, Sanctification of the New Month 2 - Restricts qualifying testimony for the new moon to eligible male witnesses. | https://www.sefaria.org/Mishneh_Torah%2C_Sanctification_of_the_New_Month.2?lang=en
- Mishneh Torah, Marriage 21:10 - Assigns wives household labor including cooking, washing, childcare and tending their husbands' animals. | https://www.sefaria.org/Mishneh_Torah%2C_Marriage.21.10?lang=en

# Slavery

- Mishneh Torah, Slaves 9:1 - Recognizes slave sales authorized by non-Jewish rulers, including captives and people unable to pay taxes. | https://www.sefaria.org/Mishneh_Torah%2C_Slaves.9.1?lang=en
- Mishneh Torah, Slaves 9:8 - Permits harsh labor for Canaanite slaves but advocates compassion and humane treatment. | https://www.sefaria.org/Mishneh_Torah%2C_Slaves.9.8?lang=en

# Cruel and disproportionate punishments

- Mishneh Torah, Kings and Wars 9:14 - Prescribes execution for violation of the seven Noahide commandments, with different procedural requirements. | https://www.sefaria.org/Mishneh_Torah%2C_Kings_and_Wars.9.14?lang=en | https://www.sefaria.org/Mishneh_Torah%2C_Kings_and_Wars.9.14?with=TANAKH

# Religious intolerance

- Mishneh Torah, Kings and Wars 10:9 - Prescribes punishment for non-Jews studying Torah beyond Noahide laws or establishing new religious practices, but not execution. | https://www.sefaria.org/Mishneh_Torah%2C_Kings_and_Wars.10.9?lang=en | https://www.sefaria.org/Mishneh_Torah%2C_Kings_and_Wars.10.9?with=Commentary

# Warfare and mass killing

- Mishneh Torah, Kings and Wars 6:4 - Requires war after rejected peace terms; distinguishes ordinary wars from specified wars where no inhabitants are spared. | https://www.sefaria.org/Mishneh_Torah%2C_Kings_and_Wars.6.4?lang=en
