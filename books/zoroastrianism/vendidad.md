# Cruel and disproportionate punishments

- Vendidad 4.11-16 - Prescribes hundreds of lashes for breaking contracts, increasing with their value. | https://www.avesta.org/vendidad/vd4sbe.htm
- Vendidad 14.1-4 - Prescribes an exceptionally severe flogging penalty and extensive ritual offerings for killing an otter. | https://www.avesta.org/vendidad/vd14sbe.htm
- Vendidad 16.13-16 - Prescribes flogging for suppressing menstrual discharge or repeatedly touching a menstruating woman sexually. | https://www.avesta.org/vendidad/vd16sbe.htm
- Vendidad 18.74-76 - Prescribes two thousand lashes and threatens a dark afterlife for failure to complete the prescribed atonement. | https://www.avesta.org/vendidad/vd18sbe.htm

# Religious intolerance

- Vendidad 7.36-40 - Requires aspiring surgeons to demonstrate competence on non-Mazda worshippers before treating Mazda worshippers. | https://www.avesta.org/vendidad/vd7sbe.htm
- Vendidad 15.1-2 - Classifies knowingly leading a Mazda worshipper into another faith as a serious punishable offense. | https://www.avesta.org/vendidad/vd15sbe.htm
- Vendidad 18.8-10 - Associates teaching another religion with death and condemns releasing the imprisoned offender, in Darmesteter's reading. | https://www.avesta.org/vendidad/vd18sbe.htm

# Women's rights

- Vendidad 7.60-64 - Requires secluding a woman after stillbirth, away from sacred elements and other worshippers. | https://www.avesta.org/vendidad/vd7sbe.htm
- Vendidad 7.65-72 - Prescribes ritual drinks and restricts water after stillbirth; permits lifesaving water but imposes a penalty. | https://www.avesta.org/vendidad/vd7sbe.htm
- Vendidad 15.9-12 - Prohibits abortion motivated by shame and assigns the deliberate-murder penalty to both parents. | https://www.avesta.org/vendidad/vd15sbe.htm
- Vendidad 15.13-14 - Assigns guilt to a pregnant woman, the father and the midwife in a described induced abortion. | https://www.avesta.org/vendidad/vd15sbe.htm
- Vendidad 16.1-7 - Isolates menstruating women from sacred elements and people, and prescribes purification after contact. | https://www.avesta.org/vendidad/vd16sbe.htm
- Vendidad 16.8-12 - Attributes prolonged bleeding to demons and prescribes ritual washing and animal killing. | https://www.avesta.org/vendidad/vd16sbe.htm
