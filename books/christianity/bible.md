# Slavery

- Genesis 9:24-27 - Noah curses Canaan to servitude because of Ham's conduct.
- Genesis 16:1-6 - Sarai gives her enslaved servant Hagar to Abram to bear a child and later mistreats her.
- Genesis 16:9 - An angel tells Hagar to return to her mistress and submit to her.
- Genesis 17:12-13 - God requires circumcision of males born into or purchased into Abraham's household.
- Genesis 47:19-23 - Joseph acquires the Egyptians and their land for Pharaoh during famine.
- Exodus 21:2-6 - A Hebrew male slave who wants to remain with his master-provided wife and children must accept permanent servitude.
- Exodus 21:7-11 - Regulates fathers selling daughters into servitude and their marital treatment.
- Exodus 21:20-21 - Exempts owners from specified punishment if beaten slaves survive a day or two, treating them as property.
- Exodus 21:26-27 - Requires freeing slaves for specified permanent injuries caused by their owners, rather than prohibiting all beatings.
- Exodus 21:32 - Sets compensation for a slave killed by an ox at thirty shekels paid to the owner.
- Exodus 22:3 - Requires a thief unable to make restitution to be sold for his theft.
- Leviticus 19:20-22 - Assigns different penalties for intercourse with enslaved betrothed women and free women.
- Leviticus 25:39-46 - Distinguishes temporary Israelite servitude from foreign slaves who may be owned and inherited permanently.
- Deuteronomy 15:12-17 - Regulates permanent servitude for a servant who chooses to remain with a master.
- Deuteronomy 20:10-11 - Requires conquered cities that surrender to perform forced labor.
- Joshua 9:22-27 - Joshua imposes permanent service on the Gibeonites for deceiving Israel.
- 1 Kings 9:20-21 - Solomon imposes forced labor on surviving non-Israelite populations.
- Joel 3:7-8 - God threatens to sell enemies' sons and daughters into the hands of distant peoples.
- Luke 12:47-48 - A parable uses severe or light beatings of servants to illustrate accountability.
- Ephesians 6:5-8 - Instructs slaves to obey earthly masters as they obey Christ.
- Colossians 3:22 - Instructs slaves to obey earthly masters in everything.
- 1 Timothy 6:1-2 - Instructs slaves to honor masters and serve believing masters especially well.
- Titus 2:9-10 - Instructs slaves to submit to masters and not contradict them.
- 1 Peter 2:18-20 - Instructs slaves to submit even to harsh masters and endure unjust suffering.

# Genocide and mass killing

- Genesis 34:25-29 - Jacob's sons kill Shechem's men and seize women and children; Jacob later condemns their violence.

- Genesis 6:5-7, 7:21-23 - God destroys nearly all land-dwelling humans and animals in the flood.
- Genesis 19:24-25 - God destroys Sodom and Gomorrah and their inhabitants.
- Exodus 12:29-30 - God kills Egypt's firstborn, including the firstborn of prisoners and livestock.
- Exodus 17:14-16 - God promises to erase the memory of Amalek and wage war against it across generations.
- Exodus 23:23-24 - God promises to destroy several peoples occupying the promised land.
- Exodus 32:27-28 - Moses orders the Levites to kill fellow Israelites after the golden calf episode.
- Numbers 21:2-3 - Israel destroys Canaanite towns and their inhabitants after a vow to God.
- Numbers 21:33-35 - Israel kills Og and his people, leaving no survivors.
- Numbers 31:7-18 - Moses orders the killing of captive boys and women who have had sex, while retaining virgin girls.
- Deuteronomy 2:32-34 - Israel destroys Sihon's towns, including women and children.
- Deuteronomy 3:3-6 - Israel destroys Og's towns, including women and children.
- Deuteronomy 7:1-5 - God commands the destruction of seven nations, forbidding treaties and mercy toward them.
- Deuteronomy 20:10-15 - Commands killing the males of resisting distant cities and taking women and children as plunder.
- Deuteronomy 20:16-18 - Commands leaving no living inhabitants in specified conquered cities.
- Deuteronomy 25:17-19 - Commands Israel to erase the memory of Amalek.
- Joshua 6:17-21 - Israel kills Jericho's inhabitants, including young and old, except Rahab and those with her.
- Joshua 8:24-29 - Israel destroys Ai's inhabitants and executes its king.
- Joshua 10:28-40 - Joshua destroys multiple cities, leaving no survivors, as commanded by God.
- Joshua 11:10-15 - Joshua destroys Hazor and other cities, killing their inhabitants as commanded through Moses.
- Joshua 11:20 - Attributes the enemies' refusal to make peace to God hardening their hearts so they would be destroyed.
- Judges 20:48 - Israelites destroy Benjaminite towns, killing people and animals.
- Judges 21:10-12 - Israelites kill the inhabitants of Jabesh-gilead except virgin girls, who are taken as wives for Benjaminites.
- 1 Samuel 15:2-3 - God commands Saul to kill Amalekites, explicitly including infants and animals.
- 1 Samuel 15:18-23 - Samuel condemns Saul for failing to carry out the commanded destruction fully.
- 2 Samuel 8:2 - David executes two out of every three groups of captured Moabites.
- 2 Kings 10:1-11 - Jehu arranges the killing of Ahab's descendants and supporters.
- Psalm 137:8-9 - A lament blesses those who kill Babylonian infants in revenge.
- Isaiah 13:15-18 - A prophecy against Babylon describes children being killed and women sexually assaulted.
- Jeremiah 50:21-27 - A prophecy calls for destruction of Babylon and its inhabitants.
- Ezekiel 9:4-7 - In a vision, God orders the killing of Jerusalem's inhabitants, including children, except those marked for protection.
- Hosea 13:16 - A prophecy describes Samaria's infants being killed and pregnant women subjected to violence.

# Sexual slavery, forced marriage and sexual violence

- Genesis 19:4-8 - Lot offers his daughters to a hostile crowd to protect his guests.
- Genesis 34:1-4 - Shechem violates Dinah and subsequently seeks to marry her.
- Genesis 35:22 - Reuben sleeps with his father's concubine Bilhah.
- Exodus 21:7-11 - Regulates the marital treatment of a daughter sold into servitude.
- Numbers 31:17-18 - Israelites retain virgin girls after killing other captives; their sexual use is not explicitly stated.
- Deuteronomy 21:10-14 - Permits taking a female war captive as a wife after a mourning period, without specifying her consent.
- Deuteronomy 22:23-24 - Prescribes death for both parties after intercourse in a city, treating a betrothed woman's silence as culpability.
- Deuteronomy 22:28-29 - Requires payment to the father and marriage after seizing an unmarried virgin for sex; rape interpretation disputed.
- Judges 5:28-30 - A victory song imagines enemy soldiers dividing captive women as spoils.
- Judges 19:22-30 - A man gives his concubine to a violent crowd; she is abused and subsequently dismembered.
- Judges 21:19-23 - Israelite leaders arrange the abduction of women at Shiloh as wives for Benjaminites.
- 2 Samuel 12:11-12 - God announces that David's wives will be sexually taken by another man as punishment for David.
- 2 Samuel 13:11-22 - Amnon rapes his half-sister Tamar and rejects her afterward.
- Zechariah 14:1-2 - God gathers nations against Jerusalem in a prophecy that includes sexual violence against women.

# Women's rights

- Genesis 3:16 - God announces painful childbirth and the husband's rule over the woman.
- Genesis 12:11-20 - Abram presents Sarai as his sister; Pharaoh takes her into his household.
- Genesis 20:1-7 - Abraham again presents Sarah as his sister, and Abimelech takes her before divine intervention.
- Exodus 20:17 - Lists a neighbor's wife alongside household property and servants in the prohibition on coveting.
- Leviticus 12:1-5 - Prescribes twice the ritual purification period after bearing a daughter rather than a son.
- Leviticus 15:19-30 - Classifies menstruation and certain female discharges as ritual impurity.
- Leviticus 21:9 - Prescribes burning a priest's daughter for prostitution.
- Leviticus 27:1-7 - Assigns lower sanctuary vow valuations to females than males in corresponding age groups.
- Numbers 5:11-31 - Requires a wife suspected of adultery to undergo a ritual ordeal, with no equivalent procedure for husbands.
- Numbers 27:1-11 - Allows daughters to inherit when there is no son, establishing male priority.
- Numbers 30:3-16 - Allows fathers and husbands to invalidate certain vows made by daughters and wives.
- Numbers 36:6-9 - Restricts heiresses' marriages to preserve tribal land ownership.
- Deuteronomy 22:13-21 - Prescribes stoning a bride if an accusation of premarital sexual misconduct is upheld through the virginity procedure.
- Deuteronomy 24:1-4 - Regulates a husband's dismissal of his wife through divorce.
- Deuteronomy 25:5-10 - Requires levirate marriage under specified circumstances, with a refusal procedure for the deceased husband's brother.
- Deuteronomy 25:11-12 - Commands cutting off a woman's hand if she grabs a man's genitals during a fight.
- 1 Corinthians 11:3-10 - Establishes male headship and gender-specific rules for head coverings in worship.
- 1 Corinthians 14:34-35 - Instructs women to remain silent in church and question husbands at home; textual origin disputed.
- Ephesians 5:22-24 - Instructs wives to submit to husbands as the church submits to Christ.
- Colossians 3:18 - Instructs wives to submit to husbands.
- 1 Timothy 2:11-15 - Prohibits women from teaching or exercising authority over men, appealing to Adam and Eve.
- Titus 2:3-5 - Instructs younger women to be submissive to husbands and occupied with domestic responsibilities.
- 1 Peter 3:1-6 - Instructs wives to submit to husbands, citing Sarah's obedience to Abraham.

# Children's rights

- Genesis 22:1-12 - God tests Abraham by commanding him to sacrifice Isaac, stopping him before the killing.
- Exodus 21:15 - Prescribes death for striking one's father or mother.
- Exodus 21:17 - Prescribes death for cursing one's father or mother.
- Leviticus 20:9 - Prescribes death for cursing one's parents.
- Deuteronomy 21:18-21 - Orders the stoning of a persistently rebellious son accused of gluttony and drunkenness.
- Judges 11:30-40 - Jephthah fulfills his vow involving his daughter, usually understood as sacrifice; lifelong dedication is an alternative interpretation.
- 2 Samuel 12:13-18 - God strikes David's infant with illness, and the child dies after David's wrongdoing.
- 2 Kings 2:23-24 - Bears maul forty-two youths after Elisha curses them for mocking him.
- Proverbs 13:24 - Endorses the rod as a means of disciplining children.
- Proverbs 22:15 - Recommends the rod to drive foolishness from a child.
- Proverbs 23:13-14 - Recommends striking a child with a rod as a means of saving the child from death.
- Proverbs 29:15 - Presents the rod and correction as means of imparting wisdom.
- Hebrews 12:5-11 - Uses parental corporal discipline as a model for God's discipline.

# LGBT discrimination

- Leviticus 18:22 - Prohibits sexual intercourse between men.
- Leviticus 20:13 - Prescribes death for men who have sexual intercourse with each other.
- Deuteronomy 22:5 - Prohibits wearing items associated with the opposite sex; its application to transgender people is disputed.
- Romans 1:26-27 - Condemns same-sex sexual relations as contrary to nature.
- 1 Corinthians 6:9-10 - Excludes people described by terms often translated as male same-sex conduct from God's kingdom; meanings debated.
- 1 Timothy 1:9-10 - Lists a Greek term commonly understood as male same-sex conduct among unlawful behaviors.
- Jude 1:7 - Cites Sodom's punishment for sexual immorality and pursuit of other flesh; precise offense disputed.

# Religious intolerance

- Exodus 22:18 - Prescribes death for a sorceress.
- Exodus 22:20 - Prescribes destruction for sacrificing to another god.
- Exodus 34:12-16 - Orders destruction of other peoples' religious objects and warns against religious intermarriage.
- Leviticus 24:10-16 - Prescribes stoning for blaspheming God's name.
- Leviticus 20:27 - Prescribes death for mediums and spiritists.
- Deuteronomy 12:2-3 - Commands destruction of other nations' places and objects of worship.
- Deuteronomy 13:1-5 - Commands executing a prophet who encourages worship of other gods.
- Deuteronomy 13:6-11 - Commands executing even close relatives who secretly entice someone to worship other gods.
- Deuteronomy 13:12-18 - Commands destroying an Israelite town and its livestock if it turns to other gods.
- Deuteronomy 17:2-7 - Prescribes stoning for worshipping other gods, the sun, moon or stars.
- Deuteronomy 18:9-14 - Condemns divination, sorcery and communication with the dead as abominations.
- 1 Kings 18:40 - Elijah orders the killing of the prophets of Baal after a religious contest.
- 2 Kings 10:18-28 - Jehu gathers worshippers of Baal through deception and kills them.
- 2 Chronicles 15:12-13 - A covenant requires death for anyone who refuses to seek Israel's God.
- Ezra 10:10-11 - Ezra demands separation from foreign wives as part of a religious reform.
- Matthew 10:34-37 - Jesus describes his mission as bringing family division and demands loyalty above family ties.
- John 3:18 - States that unbelievers are already condemned for not believing in God's Son.
- John 14:6 - Presents Jesus as the exclusive way to the Father.
- 2 Corinthians 6:14-17 - Warns believers against being unequally yoked with unbelievers.
- Galatians 1:8-9 - Pronounces a curse on anyone preaching a different gospel.
- 2 John 1:9-11 - Instructs believers not to welcome teachers who reject the specified doctrine of Christ.

# Cruel and disproportionate punishments

- Genesis 19:26 - Lot's wife becomes a pillar of salt after looking back at the destroyed city.
- Genesis 38:9-10 - God kills Onan after he prevents his brother's widow from conceiving through him.
- Exodus 21:23-25 - Prescribes bodily retaliation according to the injury inflicted.
- Exodus 31:14-15 - Prescribes death for desecrating the Sabbath through work.
- Exodus 35:2 - Prescribes death for working on the Sabbath.
- Leviticus 10:1-2 - God kills Aaron's sons for offering unauthorized fire.
- Leviticus 20:10 - Prescribes death for both parties to adultery.
- Leviticus 20:11-12 - Prescribes death for specified sexual relations with relatives by marriage.
- Leviticus 20:14 - Prescribes burning a man and the women involved if he marries both a woman and her mother.
- Leviticus 20:15-16 - Prescribes death for a person and animal involved in bestiality.
- Leviticus 20:18 - Prescribes exclusion from the community for sex during menstruation.
- Leviticus 24:19-20 - Prescribes reciprocal bodily injury for injuring another person.
- Numbers 15:32-36 - A man is stoned to death for gathering sticks on the Sabbath at God's instruction.
- Numbers 16:35 - Fire from God consumes 250 men offering incense during Korah's rebellion.
- Numbers 25:6-13 - Phinehas kills an Israelite man and a Midianite woman, and God rewards his zeal with a covenant.
- Deuteronomy 17:8-13 - Prescribes death for defying the authoritative ruling of a priest or judge.
- Deuteronomy 19:16-21 - Requires a false witness to receive the punishment intended for the accused, including bodily retaliation.
- Deuteronomy 25:1-3 - Authorizes up to forty lashes for an offender.
- 1 Samuel 6:19 - God strikes people at Beth-shemesh for an offense involving the ark; details and numbers vary by manuscript.
- 2 Samuel 6:6-7 - God kills Uzzah after he reaches out to steady the ark.
- Acts 5:1-11 - Ananias and Sapphira die after lying about the proceeds of a property sale.
- Acts 12:21-23 - An angel strikes Herod because he does not give glory to God.

# Collective punishment and divine cruelty

- Jeremiah 19:9 - God threatens siege conditions forcing people to eat their own children and neighbors.

- Genesis 3:17-19 - God curses the ground and imposes toil and mortality in response to Adam's disobedience.
- Exodus 7:3-5 - God announces that he will harden Pharaoh's heart and multiply judgments against Egypt.
- Exodus 9:12 - God hardens Pharaoh's heart during the plagues.
- Exodus 10:1-2 - God says he has hardened Pharaoh's heart to display his signs.
- Exodus 20:5 - God describes visiting parents' iniquity upon later generations of those who hate him.
- Exodus 34:7 - Describes punishment extending to children and grandchildren for parental iniquity.
- Leviticus 26:14-39 - God threatens disease, famine, war and cannibalism for covenant disobedience.
- Numbers 11:33-34 - God strikes Israelites with a plague while they eat the meat they demanded.
- Numbers 14:26-35 - God condemns a generation to die in the wilderness and their children to wander for forty years.
- Numbers 16:27-33 - The earth swallows rebels and their households, including children mentioned in the scene.
- Numbers 16:41-49 - God sends a plague after the congregation complains about the deaths of rebels.
- Numbers 21:4-6 - God sends venomous snakes after the people complain about wilderness conditions.
- Numbers 25:1-9 - A plague strikes Israel after sexual relations and worship involving Moabite women.
- Deuteronomy 28:15-68 - Threatens disease, starvation, sexual humiliation, enslavement and cannibalism for disobedience.
- Joshua 7:24-26 - Achan's household is brought for punishment following his theft; family members' fate is debated.
- 2 Samuel 21:1-9 - Seven descendants of Saul are handed over for execution in response to a famine linked to Saul's wrongdoing.
- 2 Samuel 24:1-17 - God incites David to take a census and later sends a deadly plague on Israel.
- 1 Chronicles 21:1-17 - A parallel census account attributes the incitement to Satan and describes God punishing Israel with a plague.
- 1 Kings 14:9-16 - God announces destruction of Jeroboam's household and the death of his sick child.
- 1 Kings 21:29 - God postpones disaster on Ahab's household until his son's days.
- 2 Kings 5:27 - Elisha declares that Gehazi's disease will also cling to his descendants forever.
- Job 1:8-19 - God permits Satan to test Job, whose servants and children die during the test.
- Job 2:3-7 - God permits Satan to inflict painful disease on Job despite his integrity.
- Isaiah 14:21 - Calls for slaughter of the Babylonian king's children for their ancestors' wrongdoing.
- Jeremiah 19:7-9 - God threatens a siege in which the inhabitants will eat their children and neighbors.
- Ezekiel 5:8-17 - God threatens Jerusalem with famine, violence and cannibalism as judgment.
- Ezekiel 24:15-24 - God announces the death of Ezekiel's wife and forbids customary mourning as a prophetic sign.
- Amos 4:6-11 - God describes inflicting famine, drought, disease and destruction to induce repentance.

# Hell and eternal punishment

- Isaiah 66:24 - Describes corpses of rebels exposed to unquenchable fire and undying worms.
- Daniel 12:2 - Describes resurrection of some to everlasting shame and contempt.
- Matthew 5:22 - Warns of hellfire in connection with insulting another person.
- Matthew 5:29-30 - Uses removal of an eye or hand as an image of avoiding hell.
- Matthew 10:28 - Warns of the one who can destroy both body and soul in hell.
- Matthew 13:40-43 - Describes angels casting evildoers into a fiery furnace.
- Matthew 13:49-50 - Describes the wicked being thrown into a fiery furnace with weeping and gnashing of teeth.
- Matthew 18:8-9 - Uses cutting off limbs or removing an eye as an image of avoiding eternal fire.
- Matthew 25:41-46 - Describes the condemned being sent to eternal fire and eternal punishment for failures of mercy.
- Mark 9:43-48 - Warns of hell with unquenchable fire and undying worms.
- Luke 16:19-31 - A parable depicts a rich man tormented in flames after death.
- John 3:36 - Says those who reject or disobey the Son will not see life and remain under God's wrath.
- 2 Thessalonians 1:7-9 - Describes punishment of those who do not know God or obey the gospel as everlasting destruction.
- Jude 1:7 - Describes Sodom and Gomorrah as an example of punishment by eternal fire.
- Revelation 14:9-11 - Describes worshippers of the beast being tormented with fire and having no rest day or night.
- Revelation 20:10 - Describes the devil, beast and false prophet being tormented forever.
- Revelation 20:11-15 - Describes those absent from the book of life being thrown into the lake of fire.
- Revelation 21:8 - Assigns the lake of fire to a list including unbelievers, murderers and liars.

# Deception and moral double standards

- Genesis 27:1-40 - Jacob deceives his father to obtain his brother's blessing.
- Exodus 1:15-21 - God rewards midwives who deceive Pharaoh to protect infants; morally motivated deception rather than cruelty.
- 1 Kings 22:19-23 - A vision depicts God authorizing a lying spirit to deceive Ahab through his prophets.
- Ezekiel 14:9 - God says that if a prophet is deceived, he has deceived that prophet and will destroy him.
- 2 Thessalonians 2:10-12 - God sends a powerful delusion so people who reject the truth believe falsehood and are condemned.

# Other discriminatory rules

- Leviticus 21:16-23 - Excludes male descendants of Aaron with specified physical disabilities from offering sacrifices at the altar.
- Deuteronomy 23:1-3 - Excludes men with certain genital injuries and specified ancestry groups from the assembly of the Lord.
- Leviticus 11:7-8 - Prohibits eating pork and touching pig carcasses as ritually unclean.
